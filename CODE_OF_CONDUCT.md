# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Grillo aims to be a respectful, welcoming technical community. Participation should be possible regardless of background, experience, nationality, ethnicity, religion, disability, age, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics.

This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in a public setting.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Respect different experience levels and explain technical trade-offs patiently.
- Acknowledge others' work and preserve their attribution.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries, and decisions to leave a discussion.
- Keep collaboration focused and make room for other contributors.

Disagreement is normal. Harassment is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning language, sexual harassment, targeted personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sharing private information without consent, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is also prohibited.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an established, verified contact channel. A dedicated conduct-reporting address has not yet been established. If no private channel is available, ask for one without posting sensitive details or identifying an affected person publicly.

Include relevant context and links or records when safe to share. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and limit disclosure to what is necessary to assess and address the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

If a maintainer is involved in the concern, contact another uninvolved maintainer when one is available. The project does not currently have a separate appeals or independent moderation body.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request edits, remove content, restrict participation, or impose temporary or permanent bans according to the seriousness and repetition of the behavior. Where appropriate, they should explain the decision and allow a response without exposing confidential reporting details.

Moderation should be proportionate and consistent. Technical disagreement alone is not grounds for punishment, and this policy must not be used to suppress good-faith security reports or criticism of project decisions.
